# Test scenarios: Canonicalization

Scenarios for canonicalization: DKIM's `simple` and `relaxed` algorithms for the body and for header fields, DKIM2's body hash, header hash and signing input, line endings, chunk boundaries, `l=`, and the body canonicalizer that callers use on their own. Conventions are in [README.md](README.md).

Body scenarios feed the body to the canonicalizer and check the canonical bytes, the body hash, or both. Unless a scenario says otherwise, the body arrives in one chunk with CRLF line endings. Header scenarios give the header section top to bottom, with CRLF line endings, and check the canonical bytes or the hash input. Their `b=` and `s=` signature values and Message-Instance hashes are placeholders such as `AAAA`, because the scenarios check the bytes that go into a hash, not a signature.

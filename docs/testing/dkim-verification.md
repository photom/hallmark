# Test scenarios: DKIM verification

Scenarios for the checks on a DKIM-Signature field before its key is looked up: the algorithm, the canonicalization, the version, the From field in `h=`, and the order of `t=` and `x=`. Key records are in [key-records.md](key-records.md), hashes in [canonicalization.md](canonicalization.md), and the result values in [authentication-results.md](authentication-results.md). Conventions are in [README.md](README.md).

Unless a row says otherwise, the message has one From field and one DKIM-Signature with `d=void.test` and `s=sel`, signed while the test runs with a 2,048-bit RSA key published at `sel._domainkey.void.test`, and DNS answers come from a snapshot.

| ID | Level | Given | When | Then | Spec |
|----|-------|-------|------|------|------|
| VER-001 | unit | A signature with `a=rsa-sha1`, made with SHA-1 and the key | the message is verified | PERMFAIL (unsupported algorithm): the step `dkim.fields` has the check `algorithm` and cites RFC 8301 §3.1, and the check asks no key question | [DKV-002](../reference/requirements.md#dkim-verification), [RFC 8301 §3.1](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc8301.html#section-3.1), [RFC 6376 §6.1.1](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc6376.html#section-6.1.1) |
| VER-002 | unit | A signature with `v=2` | the message is verified | PERMFAIL (incompatible version): the step `dkim.fields` has the check `version` | [DKV-001](../reference/requirements.md#dkim-verification), [RFC 6376 §6.1.1](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc6376.html#section-6.1.1) |
| VER-003 | unit | A signature with `h=subject:date`, which doesn't name From | the message is verified | PERMFAIL (From field not signed): the step `dkim.fields` has the check `from_not_signed` | [DKV-001](../reference/requirements.md#dkim-verification), [RFC 6376 §6.1.1](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc6376.html#section-6.1.1) |
| VER-004 | unit | A signature with `h=FROM:subject` | the message is verified | `pass`: a name in `h=` matches From without regard to case | [DKV-001](../reference/requirements.md#dkim-verification), [HDR-002](../reference/requirements.md#header-parsing), [RFC 6376 §3.5](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc6376.html#section-3.5) |
| VER-005 | unit | A signature with `t=1700000000` and `x=1699999999`, and time checks off | the message is verified | PERMFAIL (expiration not after timestamp): the step `dkim.fields` has the check `time_order` and cites RFC 6376 §3.5, as it does with time checks on, because the order of `t=` and `x=` doesn't use the verification time | [TIM-003](../reference/requirements.md#verification-time), [RFC 6376 §3.5](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc6376.html#section-3.5) |
| VER-006 | unit | A signature whose `x=` equals its `t=`, and time checks off | the message is verified | PERMFAIL (expiration not after timestamp): `x=` must be greater than `t=` | [TIM-003](../reference/requirements.md#verification-time), [RFC 6376 §3.5](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc6376.html#section-3.5) |
| VER-007 | unit | A signature with `c=Relaxed/simple`, then one with `c=simple/x-new` | the message is verified | PERMFAIL (unsupported canonicalization) for each: the step `dkim.fields` has the check `canonicalization`, the algorithm `Relaxed`, then `x-new`, with its range in the field, and cites RFC 6376 §3.4, and the check asks no key question. Authentication-Results reports `dkim=neutral` with the reason `unsupported canonicalization` | [HDR-007](../reference/requirements.md#header-parsing), [ADR 0067](../adr/0067-dkim-value-keywords-match-only-as-written.md), [ADR 0068](../adr/0068-a-dkim-signature-with-an-unrecognized-c-algorithm-gets-permfail-unsupported-canonicalization.md), [RFC 6376 §3.4](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc6376.html#section-3.4), [RFC 8601 §2.7.1](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc8601.html#section-2.7.1) |
